# CareFlow V0.1 MVP Scope

## Core Goal

Build a usable first version of CareFlow as a management support tool for monthly personnel planning in nursing homes.

## Must Have

### Organizational Structure
- facility / site
- house
- department / unit

### Employee Master Data
- name
- role
- status
- workload percentage
- organizational assignment
- possible weekdays
- blocked weekdays
- shift restrictions
- optional half-day capability

### Qualifications
- base qualification
- special competencies as tags

Examples:
- wound care
- injections
- palliative care
- dementia care
- night responsibility

### Planning Events
- vacation
- training
- maternity
- planned absence
- exit
- entry / new hire

### Monthly Planning
- planning for the upcoming month
- visibility of assignments
- visibility of staffing gaps
- visibility of qualification gaps

### Warning Signals
- future staffing risk
- qualification shortage
- absence clusters
- likely external temporary staffing need

### Reporting
- monthly management summary
- risk overview
- likely temporary staffing requirement
- printable output

## Nice to Have Later
- employee portal
- vacation request prioritization
- scenario simulation
- multi-month forecast
- advanced skill matrix
- deeper reporting

## Explicitly Not in V0.1
- payroll
- time tracking
- mobile notification workflows
- full-day operational dispatching
- ERP integration
- planning of external non-care services
